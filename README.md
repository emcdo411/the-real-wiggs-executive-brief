## What This Is

This repository contains an executive-level AI roadmap stress test for Veracode, benchmarked against Checkmarx, Snyk, and the Amazon Q Developer / AWS-native security operating model.

It is not a technical product review. It is a revenue and operating-model analysis designed for leaders who think in pipeline quality, retention risk, sales proof, customer value realization, and P&L impact.

The core question:

> **Is Veracode's AI investment solving the adoption problem or the detection problem, and which one is actually costing revenue?**

## Why It Matters

Most AI roadmaps are judged by capability: what shipped, what was announced, what the feature can do.

That is not enough for a CRO, CEO, or board audience.

The business question is whether AI changes customer behavior in a way that creates measurable economic value. In application security, that means moving beyond "we can detect and suggest fixes" toward a stronger proof layer:

- Are developers accepting the AI-generated fix?
- Are those fixes being merged?
- Are they staying fixed?
- Is security debt actually shrinking?
- Is developer time being recaptured?
- Can the sales team prove the value in a renewal or expansion conversation?

That is the difference between AI as product theater and AI as revenue architecture.

## The Framework, In Plain English

This analysis uses two proprietary Epoch Frameworks lenses without exposing the internal operating mechanics.

### MOC: Marketing Ops Catalyst

MOC asks whether a market signal, strategy, or AI initiative can actually move through the business and produce a measurable outcome.

In plain English:

> **MOC finds the bottleneck between a smart idea and a result someone can own.**

For this stress test, MOC identifies whether the AI roadmap is producing product capability, customer adoption, or revenue proof.

### MBEL: Macro-Behavioral Economics Layer

MBEL translates strategy into economic consequence. It asks whether incentives, operating behavior, and measurable value line up with what the business says it is trying to achieve.

In plain English:

> **MBEL checks whether the money story matches the behavior story.**

For this stress test, MBEL pressure-tests whether AI remediation can be tied to developer productivity, security debt reduction, customer value, and revenue protection.

## The One-Sentence Finding

Veracode has a strong AI remediation and application risk story, but the next revenue proof point is not "AI-powered remediation"; it is accepted, merged, sustained fixes that reduce security debt inside the developer workflow.
